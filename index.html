Dinda Gusti Ayu

Hi!, I’m interested in using Machine Learning to learn the rules of Quantum Mechanics and Simulate Crystalline and Amorphous Solid Systems.

Some projects I have been involved in:

  • random-spaces: an interactive notebook as a knowledge base for everyone who wants to apply Python in their work.
  • diffrac-refinement: algorithm experimentation - powerxrd to simulate diffraction patterns.
  • data-processing: a digital textbook and practical guide to characterise material results.
